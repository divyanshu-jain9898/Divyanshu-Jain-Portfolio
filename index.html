About me

Plant-design engineering, written in code.

I'm a 3D / SP3D developer who sits between engineering and software. On EPC projects I've customized and administered Intergraph Smart 3D for EPC projects — catalog & specifications, smart symbols, hangers & supports, drawings and reports — and wrote the C# / VB.NET tools that automate the work around them.

I also build AI products for AEC — PDF-to-CAD conversion, a Revit AI assistant and a hologram model assistant. And I've built a full SP3D toolkit: an MCP-powered AI assistant you can drive from desktop or phone, bulkload, naming-rule, property, code-list and catalog managers, and pipelines that turn Revit, Plant 3D and P&ID data into Smart 3D models.

Flagship · SP3D AI Assistant

Talk to a live Smart 3D model.

Ask questions about objects, specs and properties — or ask for changes. The assistant reads the open plant through Smart 3D tools exposed over the Model Context Protocol, prepares the change, shows it for review, and only then applies and commits it.

  • Ask · Inspect · Apply. Query counts, classes and properties, then route pipe and place valves and flanges in plain language.
  • Review before write. Every change is prepared first and applied only after approval.
  • Desktop, phone & conversions. The same tools drive Project Management tasks from an Android app and run Revit / Plant 3D → SP3D conversions.
